# Academic Risk Analysis Recommendations

The following recommendations are derived from the analysis performed on the student performance dataset.

---

1. Establish an Early Warning System triggered when attendance falls below 75%.
2. Offer targeted academic counseling after Internal Test 1 for students scoring under 50%.
3. Organize mandatory study groups and peer tutoring sessions for low study-hour students.
4. Monitor assignment submissions weekly to maintain consistent student engagement.

---

## Conclusion

The analysis indicates that attendance, engagement, study habits, assignment performance, and internal assessment scores are valuable indicators of academic performance. Monitoring these factors throughout the semester enables universities to identify at-risk students early and provide timely academic support.
